M9 + random slope gamma_i (correlated with u0i) M8 β₀, Cycle, X plot, cluster, PSU, Region intercept and X slope (correlated) η = (β₀ + u₀ᵣ) + (β₁ + γᵣ)Xⱼ + δₜ + aⱼ + bⱼₖ + cⱼₖₗ 12
M10 + random slope gamma_i (uncorrelated with u0i) M9, correlation fixed at 0 β₀, Cycle, X plot, cluster, PSU, Region intercept and X slope (uncorrelated) η = (β₀ + u₀ᵣ) + (β₁ + γᵣ)Xⱼ + δₜ + aⱼ + bⱼₖ + cⱼₖₗ 11
Indices: r = region, j = PSU, k = cluster, l = plot, t = cycle. η is the logit of the conditional beta mean μ.
Response: cover proportion y. With probability π (logit π = ζ₀, constant) y = 0; otherwise y ~ Beta(μφ, (1 − μ)φ) with constant precision φ. Family and zero-inflation are identical in every model.
Fixed: Cycle (δₜ), Region (αᵣ), Region × Cycle ((αδ)ᵣₜ). Random: aⱼ ~ N(0, σ²PSU), bⱼₖ ~ N(0, σ²cluster), cⱼₖₗ ~ N(0, σ²plot), independent; (u₀ᵣ, γᵣ) ~ N(0, Σ), with Σ unstructured in M9 and diagonal in M10.
X: z-scored median ridge cover at the PSU level; β₁ is the logit change per SD of X. k counts all estimated parameters, including the zero-inflation intercept and precision.
